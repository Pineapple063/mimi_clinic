About Us

Enhancing your natural beauty, restoring your confidence

Our Philosophy

At Mimi Clinic, we believe that aesthetic treatments should never change who you are—they should simply help you become the most confident version of yourself. From the moment you walk through our doors in Bray, our focus is entirely on your comfort, safety, and well-being.

Our Story

Founded by a highly experienced aesthetician with a passion for facial harmony and skin health, Mimi Clinic was born out of a desire to provide world-class treatments in a warm, welcoming environment. We understand that taking the step to explore aesthetic treatments can be daunting. That is why we place consultation and education at the forefront of everything we do. We take the time to listen to your goals, assess your unique features, and design a bespoke treatment plan that delivers beautiful, natural-looking results.

Our commitment to client care is unmatched. We constantly update our techniques and utilize premium, industry-leading products to ensure you receive the safest, most innovative treatments available. At Mimi Clinic, you aren't just another appointment; you are part of our community.
